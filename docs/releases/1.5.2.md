### Changed

- Keep a fixed display resolution for X11 and Proton throughout the session.
  Fit larger window workspaces within that display and show scaling limits.
- Allow a larger fixed X11 display through `session.xwayland_resolution` in
  the engine configuration. Apply it when restarting lwfa.

### Fixed

- Prevent window resizing and workspace scaling from triggering Proton's
  monitor-DPI assertion in apps such as Cheat Engine.
- Keep mouse and touch input reachable across scaled X11 windows, including
  windows positioned beyond the browser's visible area.
- Keep Auto-scaled video streams running through browser resizes when their
  rendering density stays unchanged.
